📝 Mixing Recipe

ELIXIR 35 - 99 MAD
1
Prepare Workspace
Clean all tools. Ensure 20-25°C room temperature. Gather all selected oils, DPG, and 96% alcohol.
2
Measure Oils
Using syringe, measure 4.375ml of each selected oil. Pour into 50ml bottle.
3
Add DPG
Add 6ml DPG to the bottle. Swirl gently for 30 seconds to emulsify.
4
Add Alcohol
Slowly add 26.5ml of 96% alcohol. Fill bottle to 50ml mark.
5
Macerate
Store in dark, cool place for 14 days. Shake gently every 3 days.
6
Filter & Bottle
Filter once through coffee filter. Add sprayer. Label with name, date, and batch number.

⚠️
Safety First
Always work in a well-ventilated area. Wear gloves when handling alcohol. Keep away from children and flames.
🌡️
Temperature Control
Maintain 20-25°C during mixing. Higher temperatures can affect oil viscosity and blending.
⏱️
Patience is Key
Maceration time is crucial. 14 days for ELIXIR 35, 21 days for ELIXIR OUD 40. Don't rush!
