## Mixing Guidelines

### Maceration Protocol
1. **Dark Aging**: 21 days minimum at 20-25°C
2. **Container**: Glass bottles preferred
3. **Shaking**: Twice daily for first week
4. **Result**: Oils "marry" for reduced stain

### Oil Selection Rules
- **4-Family Max**: No olfactory fatigue
- **Fresh Oil Required**: 1 fresh oil in every 4-oil blend
- **Citrus Restriction**: Citrus only in top notes, max 10min life
- **No 3 Ambroxan**: Chemical death threshold

### Quality Control
1. **Skin Test**: Spray on inner arm, wait 24h
2. **Heat Test**: Place at 40°C, check after 72h
3. **Stain Test**: Spray on white t-shirt, wait 48h
4. **White Shirt Certification**: 0 stain mandatory for launch

### Production SOP
1. **Mix**: Oil + DPG first (3min vigorous stirring)
2. **Alcohol**: Add slowly (2min gradual addition)
3. **Macerate**: 21 days dark aging
4. **Filter**: Single filtration
5. **QC**: White shirt test before bottling
