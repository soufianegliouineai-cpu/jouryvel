## PRODUCTION WORKFLOW

1. Select 4 oils per formula
2. Measure with syringe (4.375ml each for 99dh, 5ml each for 129dh)
3. Add DPG (6ml for 99dh, 7.5ml for 129dh)
4. Stir 3 minutes
5. Add Alcohol 96% (26.5ml for 99dh, 22.5ml for 129dh)
6. Fill to 50ml
7. Macerate 14 days (ELIXIR 35) or 21 days (ELIXIR OUD 40)
8. Shake every 3 days
9. Filter once
10. White Shirt Test
11. Add sprayer + label
12. Quality check
13. Ship or sell
